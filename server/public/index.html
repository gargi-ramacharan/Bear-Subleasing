Why Bear Subleasing?

Bear Subleasing is built for Berkeley students who need a safer and easier way to find short-term housing. Instead of search through random group chats, instagram stories, or unreliable listings, students can browse subleases near campus, compare options all around Berkeley campus, and contact the person posting directly. The goal is to make summer housing and lense transfers simple and easy to manage.
